What we offer

Your brand, our craft

We brew private label beers for companies, event agencies and brands that want a beer of their own — from corporate gifts and promotional merchandise to drinks for company events.

Pick a style from our range or order a custom recipe. We design the label around your brand — or print your ready-made artwork.

  • 0.33 l and 0.5 l cans, plus bottles
  • More than 10 styles — from pilsner to Baltic porter
  • Alcoholic and alcohol-free versions
  • Label design included
  • Small and mid-size batches — limited editions welcome

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?

The minimum batch depends on the format and label type — we also produce small runs. Write to us and we will match a variant to your budget.

How long does it take?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks from label approval, depending on the beer style and order size.

Can I order non-alcoholic beer with my logo?

Yes — our range includes non-alcoholic beers such as Green Hills (Earl Grey IPA 0.5%) and BEERBA (Yerbamate & Beer 0.0%). A popular choice for corporate and sports events.

Do you ship beer abroad?

Yes, we ship B2B orders to European Union countries. We help with excise formalities and choose the most cost-effective transport.

Who designs the label?

We can design the label from your logo and brand guidelines, or print ready-made artwork supplied by you.

Cans or bottles?

Both — 0.33 l and 0.5 l cans as well as bottles. We will advise which format works best for your occasion and quantity.
