Mission

Quality that comes from nature

Browar Pogórza is a local contract craft brewery rooted in the Dynów-Przemyśl foothills in the Podkarpackie region of Poland.

Here in the San river valley we combine passion and tradition with nature in a modern form, brewing beers defined by natural ingredients from Polish harvests and traditional brewing methods. All of our beer is produced in the Podkarpacie region.

Our mission is to promote high-quality craft products and responsible drinking. We support local charity initiatives and projects that promote the region.

Tradition & modernity

Proven methods, natural ingredients

Our beers come from the passion and experience of Polish brewing craft. We brew them with proven, natural methods, using malts, hops and yeast from Polish suppliers — with no unnecessary artificial additives.

Malt

Malt is the foundation of the flavour, body and colour of every brew. We use the best harvests from trusted Polish suppliers.

Craft hops

Hop cones chosen for each style — from the delicate Saaz hops in Hills Pils, through Mosaic and Citra in Herb APA, to the classic East Kent Golding in Coffee Fes. Every batch comes from trusted Polish and European suppliers.

Yeast and tradition

Centuries-old brewing methods and carefully selected yeast from Polish suppliers. No shortcuts, no tricks — clean beer where you can taste the brewer's hand.

Our inspiration

The San valley

Where the morning mist rises over the river and the fields know every sunrise — that is where our natural beers begin.

For business

Beer for your business

We create dedicated beers with custom labels on a private-label basis for companies across industries. It is a fit for brands looking for a distinctive craft product with a character of their own — from corporate gifts, through promotional items, to the drinks list at company events.

Our range covers alcoholic beers — from the easy-drinking Hills Pils to the imperial Dark Sky Coconut & Coffee — as well as alcohol-free beers, brewed to proven recipes and matched to your company's needs. We design the label around your brand so the product becomes a consistent, recognisable part of it.

  • Beers with a dedicated label in a private-label model
  • Alcoholic and alcohol-free versions
  • Corporate gifts and promotional items
  • Drinks for conferences, events and celebrations
  • Gift sets for the holidays and business events

We back active people

Sport and beer

Physical activity and craft beer have more in common than it might seem. Our alcohol-free beers — Green Hills (Earl Grey IPA 0.5%) and BEERBA (Yerba Mate & Beer 0.0%) — are a natural choice for athletes. They carry useful nutrients and work well as a recovery drink after exercise.

We are a strong supporter of sports events — running races above all. That is where our alcohol-free beers rehydrate athletes and help them recover after a hard effort. We take part in local events, organise meet-ups for athletes and are a partner of the football club KS Pogórze Dubiecko.
