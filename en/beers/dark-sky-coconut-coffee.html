More about Dark Sky Coconut & Coffee

This is Dark Sky pushed towards dessert. The same 9.5% imperial Baltic porter, only with toasted coconut and Brazilian Nº9 Fazenda Cachoeira da Grama coffee. The coconut rounds off the roasted malts, the coffee adds bitterness, and the whole thing comes out dense, chocolate-and-caramel and distinctly sweeter than the base version.

We fill 330 ml cans for a reason — this is a beer for slow sipping, not for quenching thirst. Serve it at 12-14°C instead of dessert, or alongside chocolate cake, vanilla ice cream or blue cheese. After dinner it easily replaces an espresso.

Taste

Coffee and toasted coconut on the nose, chocolate and caramel on the palate, a full creamy body.

Ingredients

Pilsner, Munich and chocolate malts, Magnum and Lubelski hops, toasted coconut, Nº9 Fazenda Cachoeira da Grama Natural coffee, W-34/70 yeast.

What to pair it with

Treat it as a dessert — with chocolate cake, vanilla ice cream, or instead of an espresso after dinner. If you prefer it savoury, serve it with blue cheese.
